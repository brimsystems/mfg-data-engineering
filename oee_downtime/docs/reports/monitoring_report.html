Section 2

MLOps Monitoring Summary

The four monitoring layers below track the model every period. Performance and target drift are the primary retraining triggers; prediction and feature drift are leading proxies; and data quality confirms the inputs feeding all of them are sound.

Section 2.1

Performance

7-day average precision each period against the held-out test value of 0.18 (16 failures), on the observations whose 7-day outcome is known. Performance is flagged degraded when it falls more than 0.10 below the test value. The share of failures preceded by a CRITICAL or ELEVATED day is shown for reference and is not the check: a month holds only a handful of unplanned failures, so it moves widely from one period to the next, as the average precision does. Across the three periods 7-day average precision runs from 0.12 to 0.93, on 2 to 6 unplanned failures a month, against 0.18 on test (16 failures), and stays above the degraded line of 0.08 in every period, so the performance layer gives no reason to retrain.
