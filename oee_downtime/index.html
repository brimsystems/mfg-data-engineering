Manufacturing Data Platform: OEE & Machine Health

Deliverables for a precision machining shop, from five integrated systems to a machine health indicator embedded in the CMMS.
Created by Brian Davis, 2026

Six deliverables trace the work end to end: a diagnosis of where OEE is lost and what drives unplanned downtime, a machine health indicator that rates every machine daily by how soon it is likely to need an unplanned repair, that indicator delivered inside the existing maintenance system, and the MLOps monitoring that keeps it honest over time.
