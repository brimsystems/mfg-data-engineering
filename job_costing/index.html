ERP Job Costing & Margin Analytics

An analytics-led engagement for a precision machining shop, from an ERP that could not say what any job had cost to a repricing review.

Four deliverables trace the work end to end: the job costing process reconfigured inside the shop's ERP and its reporting layer, an audit that finds and remediates every type of error in the records job cost is built from, a margin diagnostic built on the corrected job cost, and the KPI dashboard that keeps it current. The ERP already held the job data; the data work was limited to making it trustworthy, and what the shop sees is the analysis. Full detail is in the README.

01ERP Job Costing ProcessPrimary deliverable
Three screens styled as the shop's ERP and its reporting layer, plus the one-page process document: the job in progress with actual against estimate, each element tagged measured or estimated with its source; the close-out with the variance drivers in plain words; and the repeat-part repricing queue with the decisions taken.
