ERP Job Costing & Margin Analytics

An analytics-led engagement for a precision machining shop, from an ERP that could not say what any job had cost to a repricing review.
Created by Brian Davis, 2026

Three deliverables trace the work end to end: the job costing outputs configured inside the shop's ERP and its reporting layer; an audit that finds and remediates every type of error in the records job cost is built from, with the job costing process as issued to the shop; and a margin diagnostic built on the corrected job cost. The ERP already held the job data; the data work was limited to making it trustworthy, and what the shop sees is the analysis. Full detail is in the README.

01ERP job costing outputsPrimary deliverable
Four outputs styled as the shop's ERP and its reporting layer: the job cost screen in its two states, in progress and completed, with actual against estimate by element and each element tagged measured or estimated with its source; the Job Cost dashboard, the summary of completed jobs by period; the Job Variance report, which groups the same jobs by job, part, customer, work center and more; and the repricing queue with the decisions taken. The screens represent the reporting layer's configuration, not a specific vendor's widget set.
